# Blueprint content roadmap

The content roadmap lists content that is planned or in progress for the AI Modernisation (AIM) Blueprint. It helps contributors see what is already being worked on before they suggest something new.

This page is being set up. To suggest new content or check whether something is already planned, email [LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk](mailto:LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk).

## How the roadmap works

- Anyone in government can suggest content. See [How to contribute to the AIM Blueprint](~/delivery-management/blueprint-contribution/).
- We review suggestions and add accepted items here with an indication of status.
- Items move from planned, to in progress, to published.

## Planned and in-progress content

We are currently populating this roadmap. Check back soon, or contact the LAP AI Enablement Team for the latest position.
