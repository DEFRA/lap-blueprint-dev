# How to contribute to the AIM Blueprint

The AI Modernisation (AIM) Blueprint grows when teams share what works. Anyone working in or for government can contribute. This page explains how to submit content and what happens before it is published. Every item goes through the same 4 stages: submit, review, approve and publish.

## Who this is for

- teams in other government departments and public bodies who want to share related work
- suppliers working on Defra's Legacy Application Programme (LAP)
- delivery teams in Defra

## What you can contribute

Use the right route for your content:

- Agents, skills and prompts – follow [How to add your LAP AI artefacts to our Artefact Directory](~/delivery-management/ai-directory-contribution/)
- Project artefacts and modernisation examples – follow [How to publish your project artefacts](~/delivery-management/project-artefact-publication/)
- Patterns, standards and templates – follow the steps on this page
- Lessons learned – add them to your project's modernisation example under "Lessons for reuse", or submit a short page using the steps below
- Corrections to an existing page – follow the steps on this page, or email [LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk](mailto:LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk) for a small fix

## Before you start

The Blueprint is a public website. Anything you submit must be suitable for anyone to read. Make sure:

- similar content does not already exist on the Blueprint, in the Artefact Directory or in other government guidance
- you have removed personal data, passwords, secrets and anything that could help someone attack a system
- your manager or service owner has agreed that you can publish it
- you have a GitHub account with two-factor authentication turned on, or you plan to send your content by email

## Step by step

### 1. Tell us your idea — Owner: Contributor

Email [LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk](mailto:LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk) with a short description. We will check it does not repeat existing content and add it to the [Blueprint content roadmap](~/delivery-management/content-roadmap/).

### 2. Write your content — Owner: Contributor

Use plain English and follow the [GOV.UK style guide](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/style-guide). Follow the layout of similar pages. Every page should say who it is for, what to do and who to contact. Explain acronyms the first time you use them. If something only applies to Defra, say so. Name a page owner who will keep the page up to date.

### 3. Submit your content — Owner: Contributor

If you work in Defra or for a Defra supplier: raise a pull request (a request to add your changes) to the AIM Blueprint GitHub repository. See [GitHub access](~/delivery-management/github-access/).

If you work in another department: fork the repository (make your own copy of it) and raise a pull request from your copy. If you cannot use GitHub, email your content to [LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk](mailto:LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk) and we will add it for you.

In the description, say what the content is, who it helps and anything reviewers should check.

### 4. Review — Owner: Blueprint reviewers

At least one reviewer checks the content and may ask for changes. They check that the content:

- is accurate and useful to other teams
- is written in plain English
- is safe to publish on a public website
- makes clear anything that only applies to Defra
- does not repeat existing content
- links to the right places

### 5. Approve — Owner: Blueprint content owner

When all review comments are resolved, the Blueprint content owner approves the content.

### 6. Publish — Owner: Blueprint maintainers

The content goes live. We update the navigation, the roadmap and any related pages.

## After your content is published

The page owner keeps the content accurate. We will ask page owners to review their pages every 6 months. If a page owner moves role, they should name a new owner. Content that is out of date and has no owner may be archived.

## Who to contact

LAP AI Enablement Team, Defra: [LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk](mailto:LAP-AIEnablement@defra.gov.uk)
