# Modernisation Playbook

The Modernisation Playbook brings together practical guidance and real project examples for modernising legacy applications with AI.

## In this section

- [Overview](./overview/): the delivery approach, the team roles involved, and how the reverse-engineering and re-engineering phases fit together.
- [Reverse Engineering](./reverse-engineering/): analyse a legacy application and produce an approved Product Requirements Document (PRD).
- [Re-Engineering](./re-engineering/): use an approved PRD to design, build and deploy a modern replacement.
- [Considerations & Caveats](./considerations-and-caveats/): risks, limitations and practical advice to review before you start.
- [Modernisation Examples](./modernisation-examples/): how these approaches were applied in completed projects.
