# Glossary of Terms

These terms are defined for the purpose of their use within these guidelines. This will help you understand how they relate to terms used in business, and in the relevant legislation.

## Terms A to Z

### 2FA

**Two-Factor Authentication.** A security method that requires two separate forms of verification before granting access - typically a password plus a one-time code from an authenticator app or SMS.

### AI

**Artificial Intelligence.** Software systems that perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognising patterns, or generating content.

### AICE

**AI Capability and Enablement.** The DEFRA team responsible for governing and enabling the use of AI tools and practices across DEFRA programmes, including approving entries in the AI Directory.

### API

**Application Programming Interface.** A defined set of rules and endpoints that allows one software system to communicate with another.

### AVD

**Azure Virtual Desktop.** A DEFRA-managed virtual desktop environment hosted in Microsoft Azure. Approved supplier personnel access DEFRA systems through AVD rather than directly from their own devices.

### CCoE

**Cloud Centre of Excellence.** The DEFRA team responsible for provisioning and managing cloud infrastructure, including sandbox and higher environments for delivery projects.

### CDRB

**Cloud Design Review Board.** The DEFRA governance body that must approve progression from sandbox environments into higher environments (development, test, pre-production, production).

### CLI

**Command Line Interface.** A text-based interface for interacting with software or operating systems by typing commands, as opposed to using a graphical user interface.

### DEFRA

**Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs.** The UK government department responsible for environmental protection, food production, and rural communities.

### DPIA

**Data Protection Impact Assessment.** A structured process for identifying and minimising the data protection risks of a project or system, required under GDPR for high-risk processing activities.

### GDPR

**General Data Protection Regulation.** EU regulation (retained in UK law as UK GDPR) governing the collection, storage, and processing of personal data. It applies to all DEFRA systems that handle personal information.

### GDS

**Government Digital Service.** The central UK government team that sets standards and provides the GOV.UK Design System and other shared platforms for government digital services.

### IAO

**Information Asset Owner.** The individual accountable for a specific information asset within DEFRA. IAO sign-off is required before legacy source code can be moved or used with AI tooling.

### LAP

**Legacy Application Programme.** The DEFRA programme for modernising legacy applications using AI-assisted tooling and structured delivery processes.

### LAMAI

**Legacy Application Modernisation Artificial Intelligence.** The AI tooling and configuration layer used within the LAP programme.

### MFA

**Multi-Factor Authentication.** A security method requiring more than one form of verification before granting access. MFA is required to connect to DEFRA systems via OpenVPN.

### NIST

**National Institute of Standards and Technology.** A US standards body whose frameworks, including the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, are used internationally as benchmarks for security and risk management.

### PII

**Personally Identifiable Information.** Any data that could identify an individual, such as names, email addresses, National Insurance numbers, or IP addresses. Source code must be scanned and cleared of PII before it is used with AI tooling or shared outside the current team.

### PMO

**Project Management Office.** The team responsible for project governance, including issuing project codes for cost recharging and coordinating cross-project processes.

### PR

**Pull Request.** A request to merge code changes from one branch into another, used as a review and approval step before changes are merged into the main codebase.

### VPN

**Virtual Private Network.** An encrypted tunnel that allows a user on a public or corporate network to securely connect to a private network. DEFRA uses OpenVPN to give approved suppliers access to DEFRA systems from their own devices.

### WCAG

**Web Content Accessibility Guidelines.** International standards from the W3C for making digital content accessible to people with disabilities. DEFRA services are required to meet WCAG 2.2 AA as a minimum.
