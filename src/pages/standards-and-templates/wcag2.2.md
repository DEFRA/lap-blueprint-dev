# WCAG 2.2

The [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) are the authoritative W3C standard for digital accessibility. Use the W3C guidance when designing, developing, testing, and maintaining digital services.

## Supplier Conformance Level

- **Level A** – Minimum accessibility requirements
- **Level AA** – Standard required for most public and enterprise systems
- **Level AAA** – Enhanced accessibility (where feasible)

All modernised systems, UI components, and digital services should meet **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** as the baseline, with accessibility considered throughout design, development, testing, and maintenance.
