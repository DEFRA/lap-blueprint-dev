# Risk Management Framework

## What is the NIST AI RMF?

Published by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology in January 2023, the AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) 1.0 is a voluntary framework for managing AI risk across the full AI lifecycle.

## Key characteristics

- **Outcome-based:** focuses on trustworthy, responsible AI rather than specific technologies.
- **Lifecycle-spanning:** covers design, development, deployment and decommissioning.
- **Scalable:** applies to organisations of any size and sector, and is already adopted by UK, EU and G7 governments.
- **Complementary:** supports ISO/IEC 42001, the UK AI Safety Institute guidelines and CDDO guidance.

The framework has two parts: a conceptual foundation, known as the Core, and an operational model, made up of Profiles and Tiers. Together, these allow organisations to tailor implementation to their risk appetite.

## DEFRA LAP alignment

The RMF's public-sector emphasis on safety, fairness and accountability directly mirrors our obligations under the Civil Service Code and Government Digital Service standards.
