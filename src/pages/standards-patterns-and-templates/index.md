# Standards, Patterns & Templates

Standards, Patterns & Templates brings together the baseline standards teams must follow and the reusable patterns and templates that support consistent delivery.

- [Standards](./standards/): mandatory and recommended guidance covering core AI project requirements, architecture, security, accessibility (WCAG 2.2), responsible AI, and design-system alignment.
- [Patterns & Templates](./patterns-and-templates/): reusable implementation patterns and document templates to help teams produce consistent, high-quality outputs faster.
